Verified

Assuming, as you state, that no one could be onboarded from October 1 to November 12, 2025, the plan scaled to the days hiring was possible is about 316 hires, and the 475 actual accessions land +159 (+50%) against it, compared with −119 (−20%) against the full plan of 594.

  • Days in the quarter: 31 + 30 + 31 = 92
  • Days hiring could not happen: 31 + 12 = 43
  • Days hiring was possible: 92 − 43 = 49
  • Scaled plan: 594 × 49 / 92 ≈ 316
  • Variance: 475 − 316 = +159, or +50%

This is recorded with the brief as your assumption, not as something the data shows. Accessions by month are the better test; a one-off query can give them.
