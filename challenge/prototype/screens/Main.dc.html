Variance explanation, draft for review

CBP Officer hiring landed 20% under plan in FY2026 Q1.

475 officers came on board against a plan of 594. Attrition is low, but six in ten departures are retirements and two in three officers have more than ten years of service. Only eleven announcements were open against this occupation on August 1.

What is going on

1

The shortfall is 119 officers, one in five of the plan.

475 CBP Officers had an accession effective in October, November, or December 2025. The plan called for 594. Later OPM monthly files can add late-reported accessions to this quarter, so the gap is likely to narrow, not widen.

Verified figure. Source: staffing plan (demo) and OPM accessions, 6 monthly files.

2

Attrition is low, 3.5%, and mostly retirements.

954 officers separated in the twelve months to August 2026: 586 retired, 185 quit, the rest transferred or left for other reasons. The deferred resignation program did not touch this occupation. The workforce still grew from 26,644 to 27,650 over the year, so the hiring pipeline is working; the question is whether the plan assumed more.

Verified figure. Source: OPM separations and employment, 12 monthly files.

3

Two in three officers have more than ten years of service.

8,732 officers (32%) have served 20 years or more and 8,773 (32%) between 10 and 20. Fewer than 7% are in their first year. The data carries length of service, not age or retirement eligibility, so this is exposure, not a forecast.

4

Advertised hiring is thin and hard to count.

Eleven announcements were open on August 1, 2026. Eight are single supervisory or specialist positions (GS-12 to GS-14). The three entry and journey level announcements (GS-5/7, GS-9, GS-13 at the National Targeting Center) state "Many" openings rather than a number, so the real advertised volume is unknown. Counting only stated openings, advertised hiring covers under 1% of the year's separations.

Verified figure. Source: USAJOBS announcements open on the date.

Read before you quote this

  • The plan is generated for the demo. In use, this column is the agency's Annual Staffing Plan submission.
  • Locations are estimated, see the table to the left.
  • The quarter is not closed. OPM files through August 2026 are included; the September file arrives November 4 and may add accessions to Q1.
  • "Many" openings are not counted. Three announcements hide the bulk of advertised volume.

Recommended for the review

Drafted from the figures above. Nothing happens until you decide.

Put CBP Officer on the Strategic Hiring Committee agenda to settle whether the Q1 shortfall is a planning assumption or a pipeline problem, given that headcount grew by 1,006 over the year.

Request retirement eligibility projections for the 8,732 officers with 20 or more years before FY2027 targets are set. Length of service alone cannot say when they can leave.

Ask CBP to state the number of openings on its three "Many" announcements so advertised hiring against this occupation can be measured next quarter.
