How many of these officers could retire in the next two years?
Verified figure, with a limit

The data cannot answer retirement eligibility. OPM's public files carry length of service, not age or retirement plan, so I can tell you tenure, which is a proxy for exposure and not a forecast.

Of 27,650 CBP Officers in August 2026, 8,732 (32%) have 20 or more years of service and 8,773 (32%) have 10 to 20. In the twelve months to August 2026, 586 officers retired, about 2% of the workforce.

If you need eligibility, the agency's own personnel system holds date of birth and retirement coverage; the review can take that as an input.

Source: tenure profile and separations for this cell, standard calculations, OPM employment August 2026 and 12 monthly separation files.

Which ports of entry lost the most officers?
Exploratory, and the locations are estimated

This is outside the review's standard calculations, so I would work it out with a one-off query, and I need to warn you first: OPM does not publish CBP duty stations. Any port-level answer rests on estimated locations that matched the real state only 12% to 28% of the time in testing. A port ranking from this data would look precise and not be.

What I can give you reliably is separations by month and by type for all CBP Officers. For port-level attrition the agency's own duty-station data is the source.
