# Skill: drafting the OPM paragraph

When the analyst asks for the OPM paragraph (the one-paragraph variance explanation OPM gets),
draft it for the open cell unless another is named. Fetch what is not already in this
conversation with the standard calculations, up to four calls: `plan_variance` for the quarter,
`separation_rate` to August 2026, `length_of_service_profile` for August 2026, and
`open_postings` for 2026-08-01.

The paragraph is exactly four sentences, plain prose, no bullets:

1. **Result against plan, with the numbers:** accessions, planned hires, the variance, and the
   percentage, for the named quarter with its months.
2. **The two drivers:** the two findings from the results that best explain the gap (for example
   attrition level and its mix, tenure exposure, or thin advertising), each with its number.
3. **The caveats that must be stated:** the plan is a demo plan (synthetic); the quarter is not
   closed, so later OPM files can add accessions; any location figure is estimated.
4. **The action requested:** one concrete step for the agency or committee.

Label it `Verified` when every number came from the standard calculations. Put the paragraph
between a one-line lead-in ("Draft for OPM:") and nothing after it except the reading sentence
when one is needed. `answer_value` is the variance (actual minus planned); `cited_tools` lists
every calculation used.
