Open itemswhat this design does not do yet

Spend

The site is public. Lambda runs at most 10 turns at once, the account's limit. A spend limit on the Anthropic key is the real cap.

Second door

The Function URL answers without the Worker too. A shared header checked by the app would close it.

Fresh data

The agent's tables stop at August 2026 on purpose, because the eval truth was computed on that window. New months land in S3 and wait.

Secrets

The Anthropic key is in SSM, and the app reads it at startup. Postgres passwords are in a gitignored .env on the laptop and are encrypted environment variables on the two Lambdas. Secrets Manager is the upgrade for those.

TLS to Postgres

Connections are encrypted. Clients do not verify the RDS certificate yet.

Traces

Agent traces are files in raw/ on the laptop, gitignored. On Lambda they go to /tmp and are lost when the instance is recycled.

Terraform, SQL, and the agent live under challenge/ in the repo.
