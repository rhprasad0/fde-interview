What survived the movefrom the Databricks era

Unchanged

The eval set. All 1,720 graded rows. The prompt and skills, one hash apart. The harness and its fourteen scorers. Both apps. The source files.

Re-expressed

Eight views and eight functions, same signatures, behind a read-only role with a 60-second timeout. Imputation hash moved from xxhash64 to md5: two truth rows re-pinned, sixteen reproduced.

Lost

Genie, replaced by a fenced SQL tool over the same views. The AgentCore gateway, never built. The Unity Catalog story, now a Postgres role with grants.

Gained

Median latency 10 s where Genie took 44. No per-call cost. A $14-a-month data layer.

Severity

Customers affected: none. Data lost: none, it is public. Budget: exceeded ten times at list.

Three days on the platform, one afternoon of its pricing, one evening to move off it. The semantic layer came along unchanged.
