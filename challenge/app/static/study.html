How the session became the held-out line

The dev set was tagged and frozen first. Then the eight typed turns were added verbatim, with expected behaviour relabelled from what the data supports, and truth pinned before the first run.

About 49 of the 57 as-written failures were a row that accepted one reading of a question with two. The dev rows were written to have one reading each. A tester's words have more. Ryan approved six row corrections (A02, A03, A04, A05, A06, A08), each logged with its reason, and the rerun was reported beside the first, never instead of it. What remains is the agent: runaway answers on wide questions, lost labels, an OPM paragraph that asserts a cause.

These rows are never tuned against. The one exception, the reasoning-effort sweep, is labelled as such on the dashboard.
