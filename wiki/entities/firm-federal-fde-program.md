# The Firm's federal FDE program

A center of excellence inside the Firm's federal subsidiary's Data & AI capability that embeds
forward-deployed engineers with federal agencies to adopt frontier-lab AI. It is the unit running
Ryan's interview process ([build challenge email](../sources/build-challenge-email.md)).

## What it is

- Described in its own postings as "a strategic initiative to position [the Firm's federal arm] at
  the forefront of artificial intelligence innovation in government," embedding engineers with
  defense, intelligence, civilian, and public-health clients
  (`raw/2026-10-30-firm-fde-ai-engineer-posting.md`).
- An executive describes it publicly as "a startup inside the company" with a flatter structure,
  higher outcome expectations, and selective entry, working exclusively with ecosystem partners:
  Palantir, Databricks, OpenAI, Snowflake, Google, NVIDIA. Culture fit (hustle, scrappiness,
  mission focus, curiosity, humility, humor) is weighted as heavily as technical skill
  (`raw/2026-10-30-firm-fde-ai-engineer-posting.md`).
- Explicitly "not a traditional consulting role"; FDEs are "technical practitioners, innovators,
  and trusted advisors."

## Databricks footprint of the federal arm

Documented wins are "executive insight in days" stories: a DOE lakehouse that unified 1.4 billion
records and completed an executive-order analysis in 72 hours; a cabinet-level leader "overseeing
200,000+ employees" needing defensible insight before testimony; Monday-to-Friday federated
analytics builds; a cyber accelerator; and a 2026 critical-minerals sprint with Databricks Federal
(`raw/2026-10-30-firm-databricks-federal-evidence.md`). The parent company has been Databricks
Global Partner of the Year every year 2019 through 2025.
